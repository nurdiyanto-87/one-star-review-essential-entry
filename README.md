A deadpan one-star review of gravity, written for the [One-Star Review of Something Essential](https://make.fun/competitions/u-0xb3e689-one-star-review-of-something-essential-c7234629) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` (pure Python standard library) writes a single-line review, 160 characters or fewer, to `/output/entry.txt`.

> One star. Gravity works fine until you drop your phone face-down. Then it works perfectly.
